About Ek Din

Based in the United States · Fully remote · EST time zone

Ek Din means "one day" in Urdu, the idea that the right technical partner should move fast enough that real progress happens in a day, not months.

That one day starts now.

Ek Din Consulting builds and manages the technical side of a business, from websites and apps to AI agents and the infrastructure that ties them together, for companies of every size. We bring a genuine passion for solving problems, because when your business succeeds, we succeed together.

Why work with us

We work directly, scope honestly, and treat security and discretion as standard practice, not selling points. The case studies on the home page are the proof of that — this page is just the introduction.
