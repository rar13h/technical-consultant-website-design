About

About Ek Din

Based in the United States · Fully remote · EST time zone

Ek Din means "one day" in Urdu, the idea that the right technical partner should move fast enough that real progress happens in a day, not months.

That one day starts now.

I started Ek Din Consulting because too many business owners end up with websites and systems they don't actually control. They pay monthly fees, rely on platforms that limit growth, and struggle to make even simple updates.

Through years of working in technology, I've seen these challenges firsthand. Business owners deserve solutions they understand, own, and can confidently manage.

My goal is simple: help businesses build a professional online presence and technical foundation that supports their growth without creating unnecessary complexity.

Whether it's a new website, a migration from an existing platform, technical improvements behind the scenes, or connecting systems that need to work together, I focus on practical solutions that continue delivering value long after launch.

What you can expect
  • •Clear communication
  • •Honest recommendations
  • •No unnecessary upsells
  • •Solutions built for long-term ownership
  • •Training and handoff included
Why work with us

Ek Din Consulting builds and manages the technical side of a business, from websites and apps to AI agents and the infrastructure that ties them together. We work with organizations of all sizes and bring a genuine passion for solving problems.

Our goal is to build solutions that continue supporting your business long after the project is complete.

We work directly, scope honestly, and treat security and discretion as standard practice, not selling points. The case studies on the home page are the proof of that. This page is simply the introduction.
